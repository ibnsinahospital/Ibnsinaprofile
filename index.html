About Us

Compassionate Care. Advanced Medicine.

Founded in 2018, Ibn Sina Hospital has grown into Budgam's most trusted multi-specialty hospital — a place where comprehensive care meets genuine compassion. As a 100-bed facility spread across a 58-kanal campus, we are equipped to handle a wide spectrum of medical needs.

We follow a consultant-led care model: experienced specialists oversee treatment plans from diagnosis through recovery, ensuring that every patient receives expert-guided, personalized attention. From preventive check-ups to complex surgeries, our team delivers a seamless healthcare experience under one roof.

Ibn Sina Hospital is based in Ompora, Budgam, and serves patients from Srinagar, Ganderbal, Pulwama, Shopian, Kulgam, across Jammu & Kashmir, and patients visiting from other parts of India. In addition to the hospital, we have an affiliated nursing college, strengthening our contribution to healthcare education.

"High Quality Facilities at Affordable Costs."

Clinical Departments

Specialized Care Teams

Cardiology

Our Cardiology department is equipped with advanced diagnostic and interventional tools, offering expert care for heart conditions. We specialize in the diagnosis and management of coronary artery disease, heart failure, hypertension, and complex arrhythmias. Our team is dedicated to providing comprehensive heart care with a focus on prevention and long-term wellness.

Consultants: Dr. Shahood Kakroo, Dr. Majid

Gastroenterology

We provide comprehensive care for digestive and liver disorders. Our department is equipped to handle everything from routine diagnostic endoscopies and colonoscopies to the management of complex conditions like inflammatory bowel disease, liver cirrhosis, and pancreatic disorders. Our specialists focus on accurate diagnosis and advanced therapeutic interventions.

Consultants: Dr. Zaffar Ali Wani, Dr. Irshad Ahmad Parray, Dr. Wasim

Clinical Departments

Specialized Care Teams

Orthopaedics & Joint Replacement

Our Orthopaedic team provides expert care for bone and joint disorders, sports injuries, and complex trauma. We specialize in advanced joint replacement surgeries, arthroscopy, and minimally invasive procedures. Whether it's a fracture, arthritis management, or a complex spinal condition, our surgeons focus on restoring mobility and improving quality of life.

Consultants: Dr. Azad Shah, Dr. Yawar Haider, Dr. Sukhjeet Singh

Urology

Our Urology department offers comprehensive care for conditions affecting the urinary tract and male reproductive system. We specialize in the treatment of kidney stones, prostate enlargement, urinary tract infections, and renal transplants. Our surgeons are experts in endourology, laparoscopic surgery, and andrology, providing minimally invasive solutions for faster recovery.

Consultants: Dr. Shabir Mir, Dr. Shahnawaz Rasool, Dr. Muzzafar Bashir

Specialized Care Teams

Gynaecology & Obstetrics

Our Gynaecology department offers comprehensive, compassionate care for women at every stage of life. We specialize in high-risk pregnancy management, prenatal care, advanced minimally invasive gynaecological surgeries, and reproductive health. From routine screenings to complex deliveries, our team ensures the highest standards of safety and personalized care.

Consultants: Dr. Sheikh Naiyer Furkan, Dr. Mudasir Ahmad, Dr. Riyaz Tantary, Dr. Snober Malik, Dr. Farhat Tabbsum, Dr. Madhu Sayal

General & Laparoscopic Surgery

Our General Surgery department provides expert surgical care for a wide range of abdominal and systemic conditions. We specialize in advanced laparoscopic (keyhole) surgeries for gallbladder, appendix, and hernia repair. Our team is also highly experienced in emergency trauma care, complex gastrointestinal procedures, and comprehensive surgical management.

Consultants: Dr. Jaiparam Singh, Dr. Wasim Raja, Dr. Mohammad Yousuf Dar

Community & Wellness

Beyond the Hospital Walls

Our Commitment to Budgam

At Ibn Sina Hospital, we believe in serving the community beyond our clinical walls. We regularly organize free medical camps, health awareness drives, and vaccination programs across Budgam and surrounding areas. Our goal is to make preventive healthcare accessible to every family.

Heart Health

Monitor your blood pressure regularly. Reduce salt intake, exercise for 30 minutes daily, and avoid smoking to maintain a healthy heart.

Nutrition

A balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, and whole grains boosts immunity. Limit processed foods and sugary drinks.

Hydration

Drink at least 8 glasses of water daily. Proper hydration is essential for kidney function and overall well-being.

Regular Checkups

Don't wait for symptoms. Annual health screenings help detect issues early, when they are easiest to treat.
